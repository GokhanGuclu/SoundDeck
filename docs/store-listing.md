## English (United States)

**Description**

```
SoundPilot is your audio control center. Switch between your headset and speakers, flip
between two microphones, and keep working — without opening Windows sound settings.

Assign a global hotkey to every action and it works from anywhere: in a game, in a call,
in full screen. SoundPilot sits quietly in the system tray and uses almost no resources.

WHAT IT DOES

• Quick device switching — jump to your headset or speakers instantly
• Output toggle — one hotkey flips between your chosen headset and speakers
• Dual microphone switching — pick two mics and switch with a single key
• Global hotkeys — Ctrl / Alt / Shift combinations that work system-wide
• Tray menu — every action is also one right-click away
• Notifications — a short balloon confirms every change
• Start with Windows — optional, off by default
• English and Türkçe — switch language any time
• Auto-save — your setup is remembered

HOW TO SET IT UP

1. Double-click the tray icon to open settings
2. Pick your headset and speakers under Output, and your two mics under Microphone
3. Click a hotkey box and press your key combination
4. Save — that's it

PRIVACY

SoundPilot collects nothing, sends nothing and shows no ads. Your settings stay on your
computer. This version makes no network connections at all.
```

## Ekran görüntüleri

En az 1 zorunlu, 4-6 idealdir. Boyut **1366x768 veya daha büyük**, PNG.

Alınacak kareler:

1. **Çıkış sayfası** — kulaklık/hoparlör seçili, kısayollar dolu (ana kare, ilk sıraya koy)
2. **Mikrofon sayfası** — iki mikrofon ve geçiş kısayolu
3. **Genel sayfası** — dil seçimi + "Windows ile başlat"
4. **Tepsi menüsü açık** — sağ tık menüsü görünür halde
5. **Bildirim baloncuğu** — cihaz değiştikten hemen sonra

Pencere 660x480 olduğu için ekran görüntüsü doğrudan 1366x768'i doldurmaz. Kolay yol:
`Win + Shift + S` ile pencereyi al, ardından Paint'te 1366x768 tuvale ortala (arka planı
koyu gri bırak) veya doğrudan tam ekran görüntüsü al.
